How to use the LoveGoBuy spreadsheet

  1. Open a CAFANS listing, check the seller’s size chart, and save the exact size and color. On the product page, open Buy Link and look for LoveGoBuy in the platform list.
  2. If warehouse quality-control (QC) photos are available, compare the item and size label with your order. For tops, ask for chest width and body length if fit is uncertain.
  3. Before dispatch, compare shipping quotes for the same destination, parcel weight and dimensions. Check whether your route bills by actual weight or volumetric weight.

Frequently asked questions

What should I include in my total budget?

Add the item cost, seller-to-warehouse shipping, agent or payment fees if applicable, international freight, and any taxes or duties you expect to pay.

Can I browse without Excel?

Yes. The directory works in the browser. You can open the product links directly or narrow the list by name and category.
